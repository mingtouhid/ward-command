Ward Sync – Privacy Policy

Last updated: 19 October 2026

Ward Sync is an internal clinical duty board used by the staff of the Psychiatry Ward. It is not a public service and is used only by authorised ward staff.

What data is handled

Ward Sync stores ward and patient care records entered by authorised staff (such as admission details, medication charts and handovers) and staff account details needed to sign in.

Google Drive access (Ward Sync Archive)

The "Ward Sync Archive" Google app is used only to save monthly archive copies of ward records into the ward's own Google Drive. It uses the limited drive.file permission, so it can only see and manage files that it creates itself. It cannot read, change or delete any other files in Google Drive. No Google data is shared with anyone else.

How data is used and shared

Data is used only for ward care, duty coordination and record keeping. It is not sold, used for advertising, or shared with third parties.

Storage and security

Records are stored in the ward's private database and the ward's own Google Drive. Access requires an authorised staff account.
